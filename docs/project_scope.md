Project Title

Customer Churn Intelligence Platform

Business Problem

Subscription-based companies lose customers due to inactivity, poor engagement, payment failures, and support dissatisfaction.

The objective is to identify high-risk customers before churn occurs and recommend retention actions to reduce revenue loss.

Goals
Analyze customer behavior
Detect churn patterns
Build churn prediction model
Explain churn drivers
Recommend retention strategies
Estimate revenue recovery impact

Churn Rate
Retention Rate
Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
Revenue Loss
